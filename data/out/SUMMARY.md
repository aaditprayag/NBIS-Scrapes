- **YE2025 reported active vs connected target:** 170 / 220 = 77.3% (reported_over_target). year-end 2025 connected target (220 MW). The actual was not disclosed.
- **YE2026 billing vs connected guide low:** 257.7 / 800 = 32.2% (estimated). company connected guide, low end
- **YE2026 billing vs connected guide high:** 257.7 / 1000 = 25.8% (estimated). company connected guide, high end
- **YE2026 billing vs model connected base:** 257.7 / 941 = 27.4% (estimated). capacity-model connected base, estimated
- **2027 identified-energized addition vs >1 GW/year pace:** 893 / 1000 = 89.3% (estimated_vs_guide). company pace: more than 1 GW per year brought to market from 2027

## How to read energized base

Identified energized base is the sum of the site ledger. It is an estimate of power the model treats as live.
It is the closest series to active power. It is not active power, except at Q4 2025 where the company printed about 170 MW and the identified sum is allowed to differ by the plug for undisclosed sites.
