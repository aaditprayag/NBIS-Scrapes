- **941 is mgmt's YE26 connected guide band** (800 MW–1 GW, R), reconstructed site-by-site [E] — Filings agrees. Billing locks stay **258 / 284** YE26 (base/high).
- **Vineland proof:** ~218–300 MW connected shell vs ~82 active on gensets; T5–T9 still Bloom-gated (Capacity: Bloom delivery **none found**; Case B −~$966M 2027 rev still ~35%). Idle connected halls do not print the $7–9B guide.
- **So what:** the gap *is* the short — shells and power path ahead of GPUs/onboarding/Bloom. More connected MW without mid-term ACV ≥$20M disclosed-MW prints does not clear $243.
