The totals mix MW bases: Vineland, Modi'in and AIB are IT, and the other sites use the stated or unknown basis. **The plug needed to reach the 800 MW guide floor is ~496 MW in the base case** (Quarterly MW tab). That would have to come from Minnesota, Oklahoma, France 2nd, UK 3rd and other undisclosed sites, or from Nebius counting built-but-unpowered halls as "connected" (Vineland's 300 MW shell, Birmingham or Independence halls).

## Site detail and sources

### 1. Vineland, NJ (DataOne build-to-suit; Microsoft). Yellow/Red
- **Power reality (Reported).** NJDEP found 62 Caterpillar G3516H gas engines (1,982 kW each, ~122.9 MW) installed and running without preconstruction permits at a Jul 29 2026 inspection.
  - DataOne's letter to NJDEP: gensets arrived from Jul 2025; 16 ran from Oct 2025, 32 from Feb 2026, and all 62 had been fired by Jun 2026.
  - A Floodlight thermal drone counted ≥45 running in Aug 2026.
  - Sources: https://www.enr.com/articles/63695-inside-the-vineland-data-center-power-dispute ; https://newjerseymonitor.com/2026/10/01/nj-data-center-polluted-air-for-nearly-a-year-before-regulators-intervened/ ; https://floodlightnews.org/new-jersey-dataones-illegal-still-running/ ; https://www.nj.gov/governor/news/2026/20260922a.shtml
- **Dated risk.** The NJDEP Administrative Order and $1.07M penalty (~Sep 22 2026) gives DataOne **45 days to file for and obtain air permits or cease** running the gensets, i.e. **~early/mid Nov 2026**. The generators may run in the meantime. DataOne disputes the finding but says it will apply. Source: https://dep.nj.gov/wp-content/uploads/newsrel/2026/dataone75894-aonocapa.pdf (bot-protected; contents via ENR/Floodlight).
- **Bloom / LNG.**
  - City stop-construction orders: LNG tank on Aug 6, Bloom units on Aug 10 ("no prior approvals, plans, or permits"). https://hntrbrk.com/breaking-news/vineland
  - The Planning Board approved the amended site plan 8-1 on Aug 17 2026 (9 Bloom power areas, LNG tank and filling station, chiller building). https://whyy.org/articles/vineland-planning-board-approves-data-center-plan/ ; https://www.vinelandcity.org/Archive/Planning%20Board/Agendas/2026/Agenda%208-17-26%20%28Special%20Meeting%29.pdf
  - **No public confirmation was found that the stop orders were lifted or that Bloom is energized.**
  - Sep 8 imagery shows new dark arrays that may be Bloom pads (low confidence).
  - Bloom's largest single prior site is 80 MW (WHYY).
- **Mgmt (Reported).** Q2'26 letter: "delivered all capacity tranches to Microsoft... including two tranches over the past month". Q2'26 call: "delivered all the tranches that we were required to deliver under the contract up to date... continue to deliver the remaining tranches"; "no significant impact expected on the project timeline". The stop orders were not mentioned on the call.
- **IT MW (Estimated, alt-data power-bounded).** ~33–45 at end-Q2'26 and ~57–75 in Q3'26; ceiling ≈ (62×1.982 + 5)/1.2 ≈ **107 MW IT**. DataOne says at least 3 of 14 halls are operating (secondary).
- **Grade logic.** Tranches 1–4 are physically supported, but only on temporary, unpermitted power. Tranches 5–9 need Bloom. The 2026 date for all 9 tranches is at risk. Under the 20-F, missed delivery dates (after a grace period) or repeated availability failures give Microsoft **tranche-termination or service-credit rights**. This is a delivery/SLA risk, not a demand risk.

## Vineland tranche and billing conclusion
- **Delivered so far: 4 of 9 tranches, at minimum.** T1 Nov 2025 and T2 Feb 2026 (20-F), plus two in Jul–Aug 2026 (Q2'26 letter). The Q2 letter's "all tranches" means all tranches due to date; the call refers to "remaining tranches".
- **Physical support for the four.** Generator steps of 16 (Oct-25), 32 (Feb-26) and 62 (Jun-26) units line up with the delivery dates. Four equal tranches (4 × 26.7 = 107 MW IT, equal-split assumption) exactly fill the ~107 MW IT generator ceiling, with no headroom.
- **Remaining five tranches (T5–T9, ~5/9 of the $17.39B) depend on Bloom.** Estimated billing start:
  - Base: T5–T6 Q2'27, T7–T9 Q3'27.
  - Bear: Q3'27 to Q1'28.
  - Bull: Q1–Q2'27.
- **The team hypothesis (last tranche billing ~Q4'26) is not supported.**
- **Q2'26 revenue cross-check.**
  - The top customer at 24% of $582.3M is ~$140M, about 16% of the ~$870M/qtr full run-rate ($17.39B / 20 quarters).
  - That is below the ~$193M an equal split would give for T1+T2.
  - Either the early tranches are smaller, or customer C is not Microsoft (n/d).
- **Risk type.** Delivery after 2026 plus the grace period, or genset shutdown in Nov 2026 before Bloom is live, triggers Microsoft's termination or service-credit rights. This is **delivery/SLA risk, not demand risk**.
