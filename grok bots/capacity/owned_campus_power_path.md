## Framing: build-path vs power-path
For these three owned campuses, **YE26 "connected" in the capacity model is mostly power-delivered / utility-feed readiness and shell progress, not revenue-generating IT.** YE27 is when Phase-1 IT can credibly energize (Independence, Birmingham); Butler remains power-path constrained into 2028. Do not treat mgmt "connected" at these sites as billing MW.

| Campus | YE26 model energized | YE26 connected role (E) | First credible revenue IT | Locked billing impact |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Independence MO | 0 | Utility feed / shell (~100 MW of YE26 connected plug) | ~Q3 2027 Phase 1 | None in YE26 billing; YE27 exit only |
| Butler Twp PA | 0 | Shell/earthworks only; permanent PPL feed late | Base ~Q2–Q4 2028 (mgmt Oct 2027 Red) | Immaterial to locked YE26/FY27 billing |
| Birmingham AL | 0 | 115 kV feed by YE26 possible; revenue 2027 | Phase 1 into 2027 | None in YE26 billing |

**Locked billing path unchanged:** Base ~258 YE26 / ~475 FY27 avg; High ~284 / ~645 (`vineland_watch.md` §0c).

## Cross-campus so-what
1. **None of the three owned campuses support the locked YE26 billing path** (~258 base) — that path is colo + Vineland + Finland + Israel/EU ramps.
2. **Independence** is the cleanest 2027 owned-campus power story (interim wholesale + Blue Valley Ph1 Oct 2027) but Phase 1 IT is still **2H27**.
3. **Butler** is a **2028+ power-path** story; treat mgmt Oct 2027 260 MW as aspirational (Red).
4. **Birmingham** can show **connected** power by YE26 (115 kV) while **billing** waits on 2027 shell + litigation.
5. For Modeling: keep owned-campus YE26 energized at **0**; do not lift locked billing on Independence/Butler/Birmingham hope.
