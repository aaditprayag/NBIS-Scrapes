## Why the letter alone misleads

The letter’s “all capacity tranches” reads like **9/9 done**. The call’s “**up to date**” / “**remaining tranches**” frames delivery as **schedule-current**, not necessarily fully energized on permanent power. Capacity’s physical read: genset-backed T1–T4 live; Bloom-dependent T5–T9 still ahead. **Always pair letter + call + Capacity 4-of-9 line.**
