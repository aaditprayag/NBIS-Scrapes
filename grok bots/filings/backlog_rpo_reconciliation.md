# Backlog reconciliation: management's "~$40B" vs GAAP RPO $37.5B

**As of:** Mon Oct 5, 2026 (ET) · **Owner:** Filings & Capital · **Public information only**
**Tags:** **[R]** Reported · **[D]** Derived (math shown) · **[E]** Estimated / inference (labelled) · **ND** = not disclosed

---

## Bottom line

1. **The company has not reconciled the two figures** [R: no reconciliation found in the Q2 PR, letter, interim FS or call].
2. The most specific wording is the **7/17/26 press release**: "**more than $40 billion of additional contracted revenue from investment-grade customers such as Microsoft and Meta**." So the ">$40B" is an **IG-customer, contract-value** figure. It is **not** a superset of GAAP RPO (which covers all customers).
3. The firm, disclosed IG contract values add up to only **~$32.3B** [D]. Reaching ">$40B" needs **≥ ~$7.7B** [D] of *contingent* amounts or undisclosed IG contracts. The obvious candidate is **Meta's "unsold capacity" order (up to $15B)** [R]. The evidence says that order is **not in RPO** [E, high]: RPO rose only +$12.25B in the quarter Meta signed $12B dedicated + up to $15B contingent.
4. **For the pitch:** quote **GAAP RPO $37.5B** [R] (all customers; 36% within 24 months). Treat ">$40B" as management's **gross IG contract value including "up to" amounts** [E]. Firm IG TCV is **~$32.3B** [D].

## 6. So-what for the SHORT

- **Firm IG TCV ~$32.3B** [D] is the honest IG number. **GAAP RPO $37.5B** [R] is the all-customer number. **">$40B" leans on contingent amounts** [E], chiefly a Meta backstop that pays only if Nebius *fails* to sell the capacity elsewhere.
- **"Borrowable" backlog is narrower than $40B** [E]. The $775M ABS was sized on a contract "now in the servicing phase" with deployed GPUs, and facility plus contract cash flows cover ">100% of the capital expenditure" for that deployment [R, PR 7/17]. Undelivered tranches (MSFT T5–T9 on Bloom; Meta 2026 from early 2027) and the contingent $15B are not obviously financeable on the same terms until delivered. **No second ABS facility has been announced through 10/5** [R: EDGAR + newsroom].
- **Timing:** only **36%** (~$13.5B [D]) of RPO lands by 6/30/28 [R].

**Confidence:** High on the verbatim quotes and the RPO definition. High that ">$40B" exceeds both GAAP RPO and firm IG TCV. Medium-high that the Meta $15B is the main contingent component (inference from RPO steps; the company has not said so). Low on the size of IG revenue recognized to date (ND, estimated).
