# Open-model comments from large-company CEOs

Collected 12 Oct 2026. The question was whether Fortune 500 CEOs are saying they have moved onto open-source models, or that open models are pulling adoption forward.

What is on the record is mostly the second. Brian Chesky is the one who says his own company is running them. The rows are `data/out/open_model_ceo.csv`. A line is a paraphrase of the page named. Where that page is a write-up of an interview, the note says the transcript was not opened.

## Who is on a Fortune list

Search excerpts of Fortune's 2026 ranking put Nvidia at No. 16 and Meta Platforms at No. 17. Those numbers were not read off a full copy of the list. Airbnb's Fortune profile, updated 3 Jun 2026, lists Chesky as CEO and revenue of $12,241 million. The rank itself was not on the profile that was opened.

Microsoft, Alphabet, and Palantir are large public companies. Their 2026 list numbers were not pulled. Nebius is not on that list. Farsight is not either. Samir Dutta is here because Fortune quoted him on 5 Oct 2026, not because his firm is a Fortune 500 company.

## The comments

Chesky, October 2025, as reported by Silicon.co.uk from a Bloomberg interview. Airbnb was relying heavily on Alibaba's Qwen in production. He called it very good, and fast and cheap, and said the company generally was not using OpenAI's latest models there because faster, cheaper ones were available. The Bloomberg transcript was not opened. In May 2026, The Business Times reported him saying Airbnb was primarily using a variety of open-source models, including U.S. ones, and that the U.S. was misunderstanding the use of Chinese open models.

Zuckerberg, 29 Jan 2025, in the Facebook post of Meta's Q4 2024 earnings remarks. 2025 could be the year Llama and open source become the most widely used models. Llama 4's goal was to lead closed models. Llama 3 had aimed only to be competitive. On 30 Jul 2025, in "Personal Superintelligence," he wrote that superintelligence raises safety concerns and that Meta has to be careful about what it open-sources. On 10 Aug 2026, in "The Future is for Everyone," he wrote that Meta is still strongly supportive of open source, will build models including open-source ones, and will resume releasing some open-source models soon, with Meta Superintelligence Labs up. Reports in December 2025 that Meta might ship a closed model called Avocado came from unnamed sources, not from him.

Huang is talking about other people's adoption. At CES on 6 Jan 2026 he said AI spreads when open source is turned on across companies and industries, that open models were still about six months behind the frontier, and that downloads had exploded because large companies want in. The rev.com transcript is the source. On 3 Sep 2026 he posted that open models help safety, cybersecurity, diffusion, and sovereignty, and that Nvidia would be a home for Hugging Face. At the All-In Summit in September 2026 he said $400 billion of venture funding in the prior six months went into AI-native companies and that 80 percent of them use open models. The YouTube recording was checked against a HappyScribe transcript. In the Ezra Klein interview on 23 Sep 2026 he also said $500 billion of venture capital went into AI-native companies in six months. That sentence was not about open models. The two dollar figures should not be collapsed.

Same Klein interview: the token mix flipped during 2026, from about 70 percent or higher closed and about 20 percent open at the start of the year, to roughly 70 percent open and 30 percent closed. He did not name the series. A C-SPAN snippet has a similar remark with the open share going from 30 percent to about 70 percent, which does not match a 20 percent start, and that page did not return the caption on a direct fetch. He also said Chinese open models were by then used by 80 percent of American startups. Both 80 percent figures are startups or recently funded AI-native companies. Neither is a Fortune 500 workload share.

Nadella, 12 Jul 2026, "The Reverse Information Paradox." The orchestration layer should be decoupled from any one model, so evals can move if a model is withdrawn. The post names five things enterprises have to do: Control, Capability, Choice, Cost, and Compound. It does not say Microsoft is moving its own products onto open-weight models. A 24 Jul 2026 line calling open-weight models essential to the ecosystem was noted in the check. No primary URL for it was kept, so it is not its own row. An 11 Oct 2026 "emergency brake" comment was not read from the post and is not classified.

Pichai, 25 Jul 2026. Google has long benefited from open source, contributes to it, and has consistently made open-weight Gemma models available. The X post was also quoted by CNBC-TV18. Continued releases. No number on whether Google's own use went up.

Karp, 1 Jul 2026, on Palantir's account. Technical customers want control of compute, models, the data stack, and alpha. He asked why providers charge for tokens if that is so valuable. On 8 Sep 2026, in the Nebius newsroom note on the Palantir partnership, he said Nebius compute lets customers run their own models under conditions they control.

Volozh, same release. Organizations need scale and control of their data and models, and the partnership lets commercial clients run optimized open models on trusted infrastructure. That is the Nebius line in this set. It is a comment about customers, from a press release.

Dutta, Fortune, 5 Oct 2026. Keep OpenAI and Anthropic for some uses. Use open-source models on premises for others.

Arvind Krishna wrote on 4 Feb 2025 that smaller open-source models are how that future will be built. The sentence does not say IBM is using more of them. It is not in the CSV.

## Tags

`production` is Chesky. He says Airbnb is running the models.

`release` is Zuckerberg in January 2025 and August 2026, and Pichai on Gemma. The company keeps putting out open or open-weight models. Meta also trains closed systems. The July 2025 note is tagged `caution`: be careful what gets opened. It sits beside the August 2026 plan to release again.

`diffusion` is Huang. Market shares, and the token series is unnamed.

`choice` is Nadella, Karp, Volozh, and Dutta. Don't depend on one model, or let the customer hold the weights.

No row is a CEO taking back an earlier endorsement. No row is a date on which a company turns a closed-model vendor off.
